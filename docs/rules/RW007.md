# RW007 - Queue Lag to Consumer Latency

**Status:** Implemented
**Confidence potential:** Medium

## Pattern

A `queue.lag` change-point precedes a latency change-point on a consumer
service connected to the queue in the topology graph. The default correlation
window is 10 minutes.

## Evidence required

- An upward `queue.lag` change-point.
- A `latency.p99` or `latency.p95` change-point on an adjacent consumer.
- Temporal ordering: consumer latency must rise after the queue lag.

The rule is signal-initiated and contributes a bounded score. It is evidence
of a queue-pressure hypothesis, not proof that the queue is the only cause.

## Operational interpretation

Check consumer capacity, poll/fetch configuration, processing latency, broker
health, and whether upstream traffic changed at the same time. A queue that
is not represented in the topology cannot currently participate in this rule.

## Example

```text
14:05:00  queue.lag change-point: 8.0x on queue/shop/orders
14:08:00  latency.p99 change-point: 3.2x on service/shop/checkout
           RW007: queue lag preceded consumer latency
```
